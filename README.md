# Fasting (Sawm) — Grade 4 Interactive Pre-Lesson Quiz 🌙

A Ramadan-themed, interactive diagnostic quiz for **Grade 4 Islamic Studies** (Chapter 10 — Fasting/Sawm).
Everything is based on the textbook chapter *fasting grade 4.pdf* (pp. 45–47). No external libraries,
internet connection, or installation needed.

## ▶ How to use it (no installation!)

1. Download this folder.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** — it opens in any modern browser (Edge / Chrome / Firefox).
3. Students type their name (or leave it empty) and press **Start the Quiz**.

The folder must stay together as it is: `index.html`, `quiz-data.js`, `quiz-app.js`, `quiz-dash.js`.

## 📋 What's inside

| Question | Type | Assesses | Textbook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Which month is Ramadan? | Multiple choice | Islamic calendar (9th month) | p. 45 |
| 2. Fasting all day and night? | True / False | Fast = dawn → sunset | p. 45 |
| 3. How is the intention made? | Multiple choice | Quiet intention, not said loudly | p. 45 |
| 4. Why do Muslims fast? | Multiple choice | Nearness to Allah and His Pleasure | p. 45 |
| 5. Actions: helps or lowers the fast? | Drag & drop (2 baskets) | “Fasting is a shield” hadith; lying, backbiting, quarrelling | pp. 45–46 |
| 6. When may a fasting person eat again? | Picture selection | Sunset (dawn/noon/sunset confusion) | p. 45 |
| 7. Sawm · Ramadan · shield · Eed days | Drag & drop (term → meaning) | Key Islamic terms | pp. 45–46 |
| 8. Kinds of fasting | Match the pairs | Compulsory vs voluntary, Eed days, missed fasts | p. 46 |
| 9. Sick person / traveller may break the fast | True / False | Valid reasons + making up missed days | p. 46 |
| 10. “…nothing from their fast but ___.” | Multiple choice | Hadith of Ibn Majah | p. 46 |

## ✨ Features

- **10 questions, one at a time**, progress bar + dots, big child-friendly buttons
- Real **drag & drop** (mouse/tablet) **with tap fallback** (tap a card, then tap a basket/box)
- **Immediate feedback** after checking: chosen answer vs correct answer + explanation + source page
- Final **results page**: score /10, %, animated ring, diagnostic band (8–10 / 5–7 / 0–4),
  question-by-question review, concepts to revise
- **Teacher dashboard** (`👨‍🏫 Teacher Dashboard` button):
  - Attempts with per-question review
  - Performance by topic, common misconceptions, suggested lesson focus
  - **Export CSV / JSON**, **Print answer key + recording sheet**, clear saved data
- Answer key includes full explanations with textbook page references

## 🔒 Privacy

Results are saved **only in the browser on that computer** (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded
or shared automatically. Use the Export buttons to make your own copies. Clear saved results
from the dashboard with *Clear results*.

## 🧪 Self-test

Developers: opening the page with `?autotest=1` (`index.html?autotest=1`) runs the quiz twice
(all-correct and all-wrong student) and reports the results in the page title.
